Master Modpack Progression Roadmap

Recommended journey route to conquer dungeons, unlock technological automation, master magic, and triumph over endgame titans.

Stage 1: Day 1 Survival & Sustenance

Primary Mods: Farmer's Delight, Sophisticated Backpacks, Waystones, Corpse, JourneyMap.

  • Craft a Flint/Iron Knife to harvest straw from grass and butcher wild animals for extra leather and specialized meat cuts.
  • Set up a Stove and Cooking Pot. Cooking meals (like Stuffed Pumpkin, Pasta, Stews) grants Nourished (satiety that prevents sprinting hunger depletion) and Comfort (passive health regen without consuming hunger bars).
  • Craft your first Leather Backpack and insert an Iron Upgrade as soon as you have 4 iron ingots. Equip it in your Curios back slot.
  • Discover your first Waystone in a village. Craft a Warp Scroll to always carry a safety teleport home.
  • Press J to inspect your JourneyMap minimap and B to mark home coordinates.

Stage 2: Modular Toolmaking & Early Kinetic Tech

Primary Mods: Tinkers' Construct, Create, Alex's Mobs, Born in Chaos.

  • Build a Tinkers' Melter or Full Smeltery using Seared Bricks, Faucets, and Casting Tables. Smelt raw ores for alloy bonuses (Slimesteel, Rose Gold, Hepatizon).
  • Construct custom Pickaxes, Broadaxes, and Cleavers with specialized traits: Lightweight (Cobalt), Insatiable (Manyullyn), and Enhanced (extra modifier slot).
  • Establish your first Create Kinetic Network using Water Wheels or Windmills to power a Mechanical Press (iron sheets) and Mechanical Mixer + Basin.
  • Craft the Animal Dictionary with a Book and Crocodile Scute to track Alex's Mobs creatures and valuable taming mechanics.

Stage 3: Arcane Sorcery & High Alchemy

Primary Mods: Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Hexerei, So Many Enchants.

  • Craft an Inscription Table and Scroll Forge. Choose your school affinity (Fire, Lightning, Holy, Blood, Ender, Ice, Nature, Void, or Eldritch).
  • Forge specialized Wizard Robes that boost your max Mana and Spell Power. Imbue diamond/netherite gear with spell slots at the Arcane Anvil.
  • Build a Hexerei Mixing Cauldron, Herb Drying Racks, and Pestle & Mortar. Craft a flying Witch Hazel Broom for high-speed aerial reconnaissance.
  • Raid underground Catacombs to summon and defeat The Dead King for legendary Blood Magic relics.

Stage 4: Dragon Conquest & Mythological Slaying

Primary Mods: Ice and Fire, The Aether, Boss Locator, Dragonseeker.

  • Use the Dragonseeker Dowsing Rod to detect deep underground Dragon Caves (Stage 4-5 dragons).
  • Slain Dragons drop scales for heavy elemental armor and Dragon Blood. Multi-block Dragon Forges allow forging of indestructible Dragonsteel ingots.
  • Construct an Aether Portal (Glowstone Frame + Water Bucket). Enter the floating paradise and conquer the Bronze Dungeon (Slider), Silver Dungeon (Valkyrie Queen), and Gold Dungeon (Sun Spirit).
  • Hunt Gorgons in temples for their decapitated Head (instantly petrifies dangerous foes into stone statues).

Stage 5: Endgame Titans & The Otherside

Primary Mods: L_Ender's Cataclysm, Deeper and Darker.

  • Defeat the Vanilla Warden to acquire the Heart of the Deep. Right-click the Ancient City reinforced deepslate frame to unlock The Otherside Dimension.
  • Raid Cataclysm structures:
    • Soul Forge (Nether): Slay the massive Netherite Monstrosity to forge the Incinerator and Monstrous Helm.
    • Ignited Arena (Nether): Defeat Ignis, Lord of the Blazing Pyre, for Ignitium armor and fire dominance.
    • Sunken City (Deep Ocean): Battle The Leviathan with Abyss Blasts.
    • Ruined Citadel (The End): Face the Ender Guardian for the Gauntlet of Guard and Void Core.

Tinkers' Construct Smeltery & Alloy Matrix

Master multi-block metallurgy, fluid casting, and tool customization.

Smeltery Construction

1. Build a floor of Seared Bricks/Stone (min 3x3).
2. Form hollow walls around the floor with Seared Bricks.
3. Place a Smeltery Controller, Seared Tank (filled with Lava), and Seared Drain facing outwards.
4. Attach Seared Faucets to the drains over Casting Tables or Casting Basins.

Key Alloy Recipes

  • Manyullyn: 3 Molten Cobalt + 1 Molten Debris (Insatiable trait).
  • Slimesteel: 1 Molten Iron + 1 Liquid Earth Slime + 1 Seared Stone.
  • Rose Gold: 3 Molten Copper + 1 Molten Gold (+1 Modifier Slot).
  • Hepatizon: 2 Molten Copper + 1 Molten Cobalt + 1 Molten Obsidian.
  • Queens Slime: 1 Molten Cobalt + 1 Molten Gold + 1 Magma Cream.

Create: Rotational Kinematics & Automation

Understand Stress Units (SU), Rotations Per Minute (RPM), and assembly lines.

Power Generation

• Water Wheel: 256 SU at 8 RPM. Flow water on top and sides for max efficiency.
• Windmill: Up to 8,192 SU based on attached Sail blocks (8 to 128 sails).
• Steam Engine: Attach to a heated Fluid Tank (Water + Blaze Burners fed by coal or lava). Provides tens of thousands of SU!

Speed Shifting (Gear Ratios)

• Connect a Large Cogwheel (driving) diagonally to a Small Cogwheel (driven) to DOUBLE the RPM (halves available torque/capacity).
• Use a Rotation Speed Controller on a Cogwheel to dynamically set exact RPM from 1 to 256 without bulky gear chains.

The Aether Dimension

The legendary hostile paradise in the sky.

Unique Ores

Zanite: Tool durability scaling.
Gravitite: Anti-gravity floating ore, creates floating armor & tools.

Fauna & Flying Mounts

Moas: Giant flight birds. Tame by hatching wild Moa Eggs in an Incubator fueled by Ambrosia.

The Otherside (Deeper and Darker)

The nightmarish sculk realm deep beneath the Ancient Cities.

Portal Activation: Slay a Warden in an Ancient City to claim the Heart of the Deep. Clear out any sculk veins from the central Reinforced Deepslate frame in the city and right-click it with the Heart!
